# 🌱 Life Lessons

A life simulation game about what grown-up life is really like.

**To play:** open `index.html` in any web browser. Nothing to install. Your game saves automatically.

## How to play

You just finished high school. You live with your parents and have $300.

- **💼 Get a job.** There are **132 jobs** to choose from. In the interview the boss asks *"What can you do?"* and you pick the skills to tell them about. If you don't mention a skill they need, they won't know you have it. You can lie, but if your boss finds out you'll be fired.
- **🚿 Take care of yourself.** Eat, sleep, shower, brush your teeth and do laundry. Being dirty, hungry or tired makes interviews and work go badly. Skip brushing and you'll get a dentist bill.
- **💵 Get paid.** Paychecks come Friday night with taxes taken out, and the pay stub shows you how.
- **🧾 Pay bills.** Rent, utilities, phone, car and loan payments are due every Monday.
- **🏫 Learn.** Take skill classes, study for free at the library, or go to college and graduate school for better jobs.
- **🏅 Play sports.** Swimming, tennis, volleyball, basketball, soccer, golf and running. Practicing on your own is cheap. Paying a coach for lessons makes you better faster, and at level 40 a pro coach will train you. Enter tournaments on weekends to win prize money. Get good enough and you can be hired as a pro athlete.
- **🛒 Buy stuff.** Groceries, clothes, a phone, a TV, a bike, a car and more.
- **🔑 Find a home.** Rent an apartment (the landlord checks your income) or buy a house with a mortgage.
- **🏦 Bank.** Savings earn interest, loans charge interest, and health insurance makes hospital bills much smaller.
- **🏆 Life Goals.** 22 goals to reach, such as getting a raise, buying a house, winning a national championship or becoming a millionaire.

## Files

- `index.html`: the page
- `css/style.css`: the look
- `js/data.js`: jobs, skills, sports, items, homes and goals
- `js/game.js`: game rules (time, needs, work, money, events)
- `js/map.js`: the town map
- `js/ui.js`: drawing the screens
